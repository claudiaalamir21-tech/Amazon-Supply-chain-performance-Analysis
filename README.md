## Key Findings

The figures below are taken from the project's executive presentation and should be interpreted in the context of this dataset.

| Finding | What it suggests |
|---|---|
| **$37M in sales** across approximately **66K orders** and **21K customers** | The business has substantial transaction volume, with service reliability emerging as a constraint. |
| **54.82% late-delivery rate**; **42.67% on-time** | Delivery performance needs attention across markets. |
| **95.3% of First Class order lines were late** | The one-day delivery promise appears misaligned with observed fulfillment performance and warrants investigation. |
| **Four leading products account for 52% of sales** | Sales concentration creates exposure to stockouts and supply disruptions. |
| **Pacific Asia represents 26.7% of orders but 22.5% of sales** and has a reported **10.4% margin** | The market's order volume is not translating into a proportional share of sales; investigate order value, product mix, and cost-to-serve. |
| **About 43% of customers placed only one order** | There is an opportunity to improve repeat purchasing. |
| **The top 20% of customers contribute about 49.9% of sales** | Retention of high-value customers is commercially important. |
| **$8.1M across 14,382 orders is in Pending Payment** | The payment backlog merits review by age and value. |

## Recommendations

1. **Improve delivery reliability.** Compare promised and actual delivery by shipping mode, reset unrealistic delivery promises, and monitor on-time delivery weekly.
2. **Protect high-performing products.** Review demand and replenishment for the leading products; consider safety stock and backup suppliers for critical items.
3. **Investigate Pacific Asia profitability.** Review product mix, order value, and cost-to-serve, and test bundles or minimum-order incentives where appropriate.
4. **Encourage repeat purchases.** Test second-order offers and loyalty incentives, while monitoring repeat rate and customer profitability.
5. **Reduce the pending-payment backlog.** Segment open orders by age and value, then investigate, collect, or cancel them according to the appropriate process.
